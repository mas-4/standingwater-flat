I follow geopolitics, but what really holds me is how the news turns
events into stories, and how those stories shape what people believe about
politics. [bignews.day](/projects/bignews) is how I look into it: it
follows 145 outlets and classifies the recurring narratives in political
claims, in an index grounded in folklore scholarship. Folklore isn't only
in old books; it's in tonight's chyron.
