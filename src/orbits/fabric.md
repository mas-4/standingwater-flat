One book did it. Virginia Postrel's *The Fabric of Civilization*, about
how much of human history went into making thread and cloth, took me from
athletic jeans, Kohl's tees and flip-flops to Irish linen, selvedge denim
and Montflex desert boots.

Italian didn't help. By chapter 3 it teaches *stare a pennello*, to fit
perfectly. French wants you to be able to find the train station; Italian
wants you to be able to compliment a well-cut blazer.
