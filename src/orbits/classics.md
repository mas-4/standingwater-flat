I majored in Philosophy and Classics, and the ancient world never let go.
These days it's Rome above all, from the Republic to Byzantium. In
college, when I called a Plotinus-loving friend Platonist scum, he called
me peripatetic swine. All in good fun.

This year I started collecting Roman coins: an Elagabalus first, then a
Vaballathus with Aurelian on the other side, because a Zenobia would be
absurd. (She was his mother, and her coins are priced accordingly.)
